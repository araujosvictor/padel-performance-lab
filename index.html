How the measurements work and their limitations

The app uses MediaPipe Pose Landmarker to estimate body landmarks at sampled video timestamps. Knee flexion is 180° minus the angle at the knee between hip, knee and ankle. Trunk inclination is the absolute angle of the shoulder–hip segment from vertical. Stance width is the ankle-to-ankle distance divided by shoulder width. Lateral hip travel sums frame-to-frame horizontal hip displacement divided by estimated body height. These image-plane measurements are approximate and not calibrated to court dimensions.

Only landmarks above a visibility threshold contribute to a metric. A single-camera 2D view cannot reliably identify ball contact, racket mechanics, tactical decision-making or actual on-court speed. The report gives cautious observations and drill suggestions, not diagnoses or claims of technical correctness.

The page itself can be hosted on GitHub Pages. It downloads the pose model and runtime from external CDNs, so an internet connection is required to initialise analysis. No account, server or video upload is used. Only share videos with the consent of the people recorded.
